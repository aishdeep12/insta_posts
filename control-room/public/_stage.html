      Root Master knows every domain and creates new ones. Each domain Master runs its own team and its own work. Masters can message each other directly. A sub-agent only talks inside its own domain.

      Creating a domain
      1. You describe the work in your own words, in the form or in a chat with Root Master.
      2. Root Master creates a domain Master and writes its registry record: role, keywords, what it takes, what it returns.
      3. It proposes two or three sub-agents that cover the domain, and registers them under the new Master.
      4. From then on Root Master routes any matching task to that domain, and the domain Master routes it to the right sub-agent.

      A Master can add more sub-agents later from its own chat, or from Add an agent.

      What a chat can do
      • Root Master: create a domain, route a task, message any Master.
      • Domain Masters: add a sub-agent, delegate a task to the team, message other Masters. Social Master can also start the Instagram mission.
      • Sub-agents: answer within their speciality and message their own Master or teammates. Scout, Maker and Poster can also change the brief, caption, hashtags and timing.

      Messages between agents show in the event log and in both agents' chats.
